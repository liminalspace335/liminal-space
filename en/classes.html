04 — THE PROCESS

The Class Process

Duration 1–2 hours · By reservation only.

LIMINAL SPACE is not a DIY where you simply mix scents. We interpret how fragrance interacts with people, personality, weather and circumstance, running the workshop so you can create the scent you truly want.

Make a special memory in the one-of-a-kind space that is LIMINAL SPACE.

01

Consultation

Through a 1:1 consultation and guided session with a scent designer, we discover the scents you love together.

02

Scent testing

Freely sample over 100 scents in our scent room and bring your preferences into focus.

03

Fragrance design

Using the notes you chose, we design a scent that is uniquely yours — your scent designer guides you so it fits your taste perfectly.

04

Blending

Through several rounds of blending, you refine and complete your very own perfume.

05

Packaging

We carefully wrap it in LIMINAL SPACE's own signature packaging.
